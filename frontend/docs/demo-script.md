# 2-Minute Product Demo Script

**Preparation:** 
1. Open the app (`npm run dev`).
2. Go to the Admin Dashboard and click **"Start Presentation Demo"** to ensure the app is in the Day 4 (317 registrations) state.
3. Keep the screen recording software ready.

**[0:00 - 0:15] Landing Page & Conversion**
* **Action:** Start on the home page (`/`).
* **Speak:** "This is the optimized landing page. Notice the focus on 'Portfolio Ready' over theoretical learning. I'm going to register as a demo student."
* **Action:** Click "Register Free", fill in a dummy name and email, and hit Submit.

**[0:15 - 0:45] The Viral Loop (Dashboard)**
* **Action:** The app redirects to `/dashboard`.
* **Speak:** "Instead of a simple 'Thank You' page, the student enters a referral loop. They instantly receive a unique tracking link, a QR code for physical sharing in colleges, and a 1-click WhatsApp button. You can also see the gamified milestone tracker that pushes them to invite more friends."

**[0:45 - 1:10] Admin Command Center**
* **Action:** Click "Growth Admin" in the top right header.
* **Speak:** "But the real product is the Campaign Command Center. Here we track our velocity. We're currently at 317 simulated registrations. Let's look at how we hit 500."
* **Action:** Click the "Path to 500" tab.
* **Speak:** "This shows our modeled contribution by channel. Now let's test these assumptions."

**[1:10 - 1:35] Campaign Simulator**
* **Action:** Click the "Simulator" tab. Make sure it's on "Base Case".
* **Speak:** "Because ₹2,000 can't buy 500 leads, I built this simulator. Watch what happens if I increase our assumed Ambassador Count from 25 to 40."
* **Action:** Change the Ambassador input to 40. The Net Modeled Regs crosses 500 and turns green.
* **Speak:** "The model immediately proves that focusing on ambassador activation is our fastest path to 500."

**[1:35 - 2:00] AI Copilot & Wrap Up**
* **Action:** Click the "AI Copilot" tab.
* **Speak:** "The AI Copilot diagnoses this exact bottleneck automatically and recommends reallocating budget to incentives. It even allows you to simulate the action directly."
* **Action:** Click "Simulate Action". (It jumps back to the simulator).
* **Speak:** "Finally, all of this data is exportable and trackable through A/B testing infrastructure. This proves I don't just guess at growth—I model it, track it, and optimize it. Thank you."
