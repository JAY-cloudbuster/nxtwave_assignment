# AI Workshop Growth Plan (5-Slide Structure)

## SLIDE 1: THE STUDENT (Target Insight)
**Target:** Final-year engineering students.
**Motivation:** Placements are approaching, and they need a strong resume.
**Barrier:** Lack of time and practical experience.
**Registration Trigger:** "Build a portfolio-ready AI project in 60 minutes."
**Sharing Trigger:** Gamified referral milestones offering exclusive campaign perks (simulated).

## SLIDE 2: THE GROWTH LOOP
**Mechanism (The Viral Loop):**
1. **Discovery:** User lands on the workshop page via paid seed or organic reach.
2. **Registration:** Frictionless form, zero cost.
3. **Referral:** Immediately granted a unique tracking link, QR code, and 1-click WhatsApp share button.
4. **Distribution:** User shares the link in college WhatsApp groups and tech clubs.
5. **Friend Registration:** A friend clicks and registers (Attribution loop closes).
6. **Reward:** Original user's dashboard progress bar increases.

## SLIDE 3: PATH TO 500 (Bottom-up Model)
To reach 500 registrations with ₹2,000, we cannot rely on raw paid ads. We use the budget strictly for a "seed" audience and rely on product-led growth to hit the target.

| Channel | Assumption / Calculation | Modeled Registrations |
|---------|---------------------------|------------------------|
| **Paid Seed** | ₹1,200 budget / ₹40 CPA | 30 |
| **Email/Organic** | 5,000 reach × 2% conversion | 100 |
| **College Communities** | 10 communities × 200 members × 5% conversion | 100 |
| **Student Ambassadors** | 25 active ambassadors × 150 reach × 15% CTR × 10% Conv × 15% Overlap Adj | ~270 |
| **Total** | | **500** |

*Budget Allocation (₹2,000): Paid Seed (₹1,200), Creatives (₹300), Ambassador Incentives (₹400), Contingency (₹100).*

## SLIDE 4: WHAT I BUILT (The AI Growth Engine)
Instead of just mocking up a landing page, I built a functional **7-Day Campaign Operating System**. 
* **The Student Experience:** A registration portal and gamified referral dashboard with WhatsApp/QR sharing to drive acquisition.
* **The Operator Experience:** A Command Center featuring a Campaign Simulator, Funnel Analysis, AI Copilot diagnosis, and A/B Testing infrastructure to model the exact math to 500 registrations.

## SLIDE 5: JUDGMENT & LEARNING
**What changed between my first idea and the final solution?**
I initially planned to build a heavily optimized landing page. I realized that 500 leads for ₹2,000 requires an impossible ₹4 CPA. I pivoted the entire strategy to a Referral Engine to mathematically solve the constraint.

**What would improve with another 24 hours?**
I would implement an automated email/WhatsApp drip sequence that triggers when a student is exactly 1 referral away from a milestone, maximizing the loop efficiency.

**What did AI suggest that was rejected and why?**
AI suggested spreading the ₹2,000 budget across Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads, and Google Search. I rejected this. Given the low budget, spreading it too thin guarantees zero algorithmic learning. I allocated the budget entirely to highly targeted "seed" acquisition.
